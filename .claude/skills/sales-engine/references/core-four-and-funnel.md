# Core Four, lead magnets, funnel design and tripwire gates

Funnel-mode mechanics, summarized from Alex Hormozi's *$100M Leads*. Tune every example to the project.

## The Core Four: the only four ways to get leads

| Channel | What it is | Reach | Use it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm outreach | People who already know you: network, past contacts, referrals | Warm, one-to-one | Default first move. Highest conversion, lowest cost. |
| Cold outreach | Strangers one-to-one (e-mail, DM, phone) | Cold, one-to-one | Once the message and the ideal customer are tight. Check the cold-contact rules for the buyer's country first. |
| Content | Free value in public (posts, video, articles) | Warm-ish, one-to-many | Compounds over time and builds proof and inbound. |
| Paid ads | Paying to reach strangers | Cold, one-to-many | Only with budget and a proven offer to scale. Usually last. |

**Audit, do not add blindly.** Find the one underused channel with the most leverage now rather than spreading thin. Stack channels only when one already works.

**The Rule of 100 (effort, not outcome):** a daily activity bar, such as 100 warm reach-outs, 100 minutes of content or 100 sends, so lead generation is a habit and not a mood.

**Give until they ask.** Lead with real value (an intro, an insight, help) before any pitch. The first touch is not a sale.

## Lead magnets

A lead magnet is a specific, free, complete-feeling deliverable that solves a narrow real problem, asks for an e-mail address, and sets up the paid offer.

1. **Narrow and real:** solves one sharp problem fully (a checklist, a one-process audit), not a vague "free guide".
2. **Reveals the bigger problem:** solving the small thing shows the need for the paid transformation.
3. **Gated by e-mail:** capture is the point; it starts the nurture sequence.
4. **Fast value:** the win arrives immediately, which lowers Time Delay in the Value Equation.

## Funnel design: awareness, capture, nurture, convert

1. **Awareness:** a Core Four channel brings the right person into view.
2. **Capture:** the lead magnet, or a direct reply, gets the address or starts the conversation.
3. **Nurture:** a sequence (e-mails, content, a community) that builds trust and raises Perceived Likelihood.
4. **Convert:** the offer (`/offer-design`) or the booked call (CLOSER, call mode). Keep the call to action low-friction.

Map each stage for the project: the channel, the magnet, the nurture mechanism and the conversion event.

## Tripwire gates: pre-committed kill or scale decisions

Set these before any test starts:
- **Metric:** the one number that means it is working (sign-ups, qualified replies, paid conversions). Not vanity views unless views really are the leading indicator.
- **Bar:** the threshold.
- **Deadline:** when you check.
- **Decision:** scale, iterate once more, or kill. Pre-commit it so sunk cost cannot override it later.

Write the gate as one line. Neutral example: *"Free intro workshop: at least 10 e-mail sign-ups by week 6, then run the paid series; else kill it and put the hours back into referrals."*

Protect the channel that already converts while a speculative test runs. A low-probability experiment must never starve the working track.
