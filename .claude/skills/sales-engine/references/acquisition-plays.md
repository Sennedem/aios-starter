# Acquisition plays: what to run, in what order, and what Claude does

Operating rules for funnel mode.

## The order (every project)

1. **Leak audit before any new channel.** Answer three questions from live data:
   - **Speed:** how long from an inquiry, comment or DM to the first human answer? Aim for an automatic acknowledgement within minutes and a human contact the same day. Lead-response research shows the odds of qualifying a lead fall sharply within the first half hour.
   - **Capture:** of the people who already see the offer (site visitors, viewers, profile visits), how many get a clear next step, and does that path work end to end? Test the form or DM path yourself.
   - **Follow-up:** does every lead get the agreed second touch?
   Fix the leaks first. They cost nothing and multiply every channel added later.
2. **Warm and borrowed trust:** referrals, reviews, marketplaces where buyers already search, the user's own network.
3. **Direct one-to-one, where legal:** phone, e-mail or DM to a defined list. Check the cold-contact rules for the buyer's country before any cold send; consumer and business rules often differ.
4. **Compounding:** content, search pages, community. Slow; start early, judge late.
5. **Paid:** only as a capped cost test, and only when CAC is below a third of LTV on real numbers. Compute LTV first (price × median customer lifetime); if the lifetime is unknown, paid waits.

## Plays by business type

### Local or online service (consumers pay per session or package)
| Play | What makes it work |
|---|---|
| Two-sided referral | Reward both sides in service, not cash; the referrer earns it after the referred customer's first paid session; ask right after a visible win; put the link in every report or invoice. |
| Reviews | Ask at the end of every package and after a good result. Build a solid base of reviews before paying for search ads. |
| Marketplace profile | Free first contact, fast replies (marketplaces rank on response time), complete profile and badges. |
| Fast call-back | An auto-reply with two concrete call moments, a human call the same day. Lifts every channel. |
| Seasonal push | Start 3 to 6 weeks before the moments customers feel the need most. Timing, not a channel. |

### B2B service
| Play | What makes it work |
|---|---|
| Phone to businesses | A local number, a context opener and the reason for the call; sell a 15-minute meeting, not the service; several attempts per number at the times decision makers pick up. The list is usually the bottleneck. |
| Warm doors | The user's network, existing clients' contacts, industry associations as referral routes. Fastest yes, low volume. |
| A reason to buy now | A deadline or obligation the buyer already has (a compliance date, a training requirement). Verify it with the source before promising it. |
| LinkedIn warm-up | By hand: follow and comment a few times, connect with a non-pitch note, pitch only after they engage. |
| Pay-after or first clients free | Criteria and the later price agreed in writing first; cap the free ones; at the end book the next paid session and ask for a named quote. Treat it as a test. |

### Community or subscription
| Play | What makes it work |
|---|---|
| Capture fix | A clear call to action on every piece of content; send the link by DM to everyone who asks; one link on the profile. Never require a follow to get content. |
| Dated cohort plus trial | "Starts on <date>" beats an open monthly ask; a trial can lift free-to-paid conversion. |
| Higher-intent source | Search-driven content (people searching for the exact problem) converts better than broad reach. |
| Paid | Only once LTV is about three times the cost per paying member. |

## Claude's jobs (the user approves every one)

| Job | Claude does | The user does |
|---|---|---|
| Speed to lead | Drafts the instant acknowledgement with two call slots; a same-day list of who is waiting | Calls |
| Referral and review engine | Flags win moments from project notes, drafts the ask | Sends it |
| Phone prep | Builds the call list (checks each entry is a business, not a consumer), a one-page brief per prospect, the opener, a call log, the follow-up mail | Dials |
| Customer re-engagement | Segments past customers, drafts a relevant offer with an opt-out | Approves and sends |
| Intent monitoring | Finds "who can help with X" threads and drafts a useful answer | Reads the thread, posts it themselves |
| Search pages | A few fact-dense pages on the questions buyers ask | Fact-checks, approves publishing |
| Reply triage and trackers | Classifies replies, records opt-outs, keeps the channel list current | Answers the warm ones |

**Never:** automated actions on the user's LinkedIn account (account restriction), agent posting on forums or undisclosed AI personas (bans), mass-generated pages (search penalties), AI chat with customers that does not say it is AI, or any send the user has not reviewed.

## Gates
Every play that is a test gets the tripwire line from `core-four-and-funnel.md` before it starts: metric, bar, date, decision.
