# Broad-appeal vs niche-appeal shorts

On a brand-new channel, the videos that break out are the ones that work on anyone: content that does not need YouTube to have figured out who your audience is. The cited example is general animation and universal-curiosity channels that blow up instantly while specialist channels grind.

Source: [[wiki/sources/dmscales-shorts-algorithm|dmscales, "The Entire Shorts Algorithm Explained in 180 Seconds"]] (01:18-01:28). Graded plausible: mechanically sensible, not platform-confirmed.

## The mechanism

A new channel has no audience profile, so the platform serves its early shorts to a poorly targeted test audience (the [[wiki/concepts/seed-audience-testing|seed audience]]). Two kinds of video meet that audience:

- **Niche-appeal** only lands if the viewer already cares about the topic. On an untargeted audience most viewers swipe, [[wiki/concepts/shorts-retention-vvsa-avd|VVSA]] collapses, and the video is judged badly even when it is good for its intended viewer.
- **Broad-appeal** lands on anyone. It survives bad targeting, posts strong retention, and earns wider distribution.

This is a cold-start problem, and it ends: once the platform has learned who responds, niche content gets served to the right people and performs.

## Sequence them

Broad and niche appeal are sequenced, and both pieces of advice are true at different stages:

- **Before targeting converges**, broad appeal is what escapes [[wiki/concepts/30k-view-jail|30k view jail]], because it does not need data.
- **After it converges**, niche specificity is what builds [[wiki/concepts/viewer-tier-conversion|regular viewers]], and an oversaturated niche is the hardest place to grow.

Leading with niche-appeal content on a zero-data channel means being judged by people who were never the audience. The trap: a specialist brand's most obviously on-brand content is usually its most niche-appeal content, so the instinct is backwards at the start.

The practical reading is to keep the niche and widen the entry point: find the framing of your topic that a stranger with no interest in it would still watch.

TikTok advice reaches a similar place from another direction: post on formats and topics that are already circulating. See [[wiki/concepts/tiktok-length-and-format-matching|TikTok length and format matching]].
