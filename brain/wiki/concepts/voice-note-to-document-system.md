# Voice note to document system

System 1 of [[wiki/sources/nateherk-4-claude-systems-that-sell|Nate Herk's four Claude systems that sell]] [00:24-02:11]. A field worker sends a voice note and gets back a finished, branded document. It is the simplest of the four to build.

- **Problem:** foremen must complete site registers, risk assessments and plant registers. Nobody types them up after a long day, so paperwork runs days behind the work, which is a safety and compliance risk.
- **Buyer:** a construction company owner. Nate's targeting advice: trades where people are not at a desk (construction, plumbing, electrical). Ask which document the team hates most and automate only that one first.
- **Build outline (as described):** the foreman sends a WhatsApp voice note; it is transcribed, matched to the project's job record, turned into a branded PDF, saved to SharePoint and sent to whoever needs it.
- **Human gate:** the output is a draft that a person reviews and signs off [00:59-01:02]. See [[wiki/concepts/automation-boundary-human-in-the-loop|the automation boundary]].
- **Price:** £4,500 (about $6,000), sold by a community member, Jodie. Pitch math: 5 foremen x 5 hours = 25 paid hours a week, plus the compliance risk [01:10-01:25]. See [[wiki/techniques/pitch-ai-systems-on-business-outcome|Pitch AI systems on business outcome]].
- **Why change management is cheap:** everyone already knows how to send a voice note and what goes in the document [01:32-01:39].
- **Proof grade:** creator-claimed; no invoice or client name in the transcript.
- **Fits:** supply-constrained businesses. See [[wiki/concepts/demand-vs-supply-constrained-business|Demand vs supply constrained business]].
