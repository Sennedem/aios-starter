# Trial reels and duplicate detection

**Trial reels** are Instagram reels shown first to non-followers only, so a post can be tested without exposing existing followers.

**Claim** (13:42-15:49 in [[wiki/sources/buildyourtribe-instagram-algorithm-2026|Build Your Tribe]]): Instagram is cracking down on reposting the same trial reel again and again to farm non-follower views. A change that used to count as a new trial reel, the same B-roll with different text on top, is now treated as a duplicate. A reel needs "a significant change or a large variation" to count as original. The host says he will cover how to work around it in his next episode.

**Grade: creator-empirical, unverified.** An observation from his account and "many others" he works with; no Instagram statement found. The follow-up episode is not covered here, so the workaround is unknown.

## What is claimed and what is not

- Claimed: swapping the text on identical footage is flagged as a duplicate in trial mode.
- Not claimed: how large "significant" is, whether the same detector applies to normal (non-trial) reels, or whether the penalty is lower reach or removal from trial.
- Related (different sources, secondary only): blogs say frequent reposts of the same media can drop an account from Explore and Reels recommendation; figures unverified.

## Who this hits

An account that varies reels only by on-screen text over reused footage, a template factory, is the exact pattern described. A distinct script, spoken line and scene is a far bigger change than a text swap.
