# Demand-constrained vs supply-constrained business

A one-question diagnosis Nate Herk uses before prescribing an AI system **[10:09-10:37]** ([[wiki/sources/nateherk-4-claude-systems-that-sell|source]]). The frame: attack the client's actual constraint, "like we did running the agency", instead of productizing one favourite solution.

| Business is... | Needs | Fitting system | Wrong system |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Demand constrained** (not enough customers) | More leads and conversion | [[wiki/concepts/creator-os-system\|Creator OS]], [[wiki/concepts/intake-and-follow-up-funnel-system\|intake funnel]] | Capacity tools |
| **Supply constrained** (more work than capacity) | Time back, capacity | [[wiki/concepts/voice-note-to-document-system\|Voice note to document]], [[wiki/concepts/company-brain-system\|company brain]] | Lead generation: "just adds more stress" and makes the process less efficient |

Practical use: the first discovery question is "do you have more work than you can handle, or not enough customers?". It filters which of the four systems to pitch, and gives a reason to walk away from a mismatched sale. A creator-stated heuristic, not tested.
