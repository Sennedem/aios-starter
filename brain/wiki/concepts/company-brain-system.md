# Company brain system

System 4 of [[wiki/sources/nateherk-4-claude-systems-that-sell|Nate Herk's four]] **[06:34-09:11]**, the highest priced and, per Nate, the highest in demand. An AI that knows what the business knows (documents, processes, client history), so anyone on the team can ask and get an accurate, current answer. It is the [[wiki/concepts/claude-md-as-router|router]] plus [[wiki/concepts/llm-wiki-pattern|wiki]] pattern sold as a client deliverable.

- **Build outline:** connect the tools where answers live (Drive, Calendar, Gmail, Asana, HubSpot); add daily briefings and project recaps; file each meeting transcript into the right folder. Once the base exists, layer role-based views, document generators, per-lead research briefs and Claude Code routines on top [08:28-08:34, 08:44-08:49].
- **Pitch:** the hours the team loses searching. 20 people x 2 hours = 40 payroll hours a week; plus faster onboarding of new hires [07:23-07:31]. The cited "study" (about 50% of time spent on the scavenger hunt) is unsourced.
- **Stickiness:** once a team relies on it, they will not turn it off [07:46-07:52]. Hidden cost: change management, since the team needs time to reach for it first [07:53-08:05].
- **Delivery advice:** build one for yourself first as living proof; pick one business type; collect the questions the team asks weekly; connect only the tools needed; reuse the organizing approach across clients [08:10-08:28].
- **Pricing modes (creator-claimed):** a template at $15,000, 17 sold, 9 of 17 on monthly plans ("Janette"); custom builds at $2,000 per client in different countries ("Kieran"), reportedly hired to build 30 for one company [08:37-09:09]. The "$137" income figure in the captions is garbled; do not cite it.
- **Fits:** supply-constrained businesses (see the [[wiki/concepts/demand-vs-supply-constrained-business|constraint test]]).
- **Security note (not in the video):** connecting Gmail and HubSpot to one assistant concentrates customer data; the video does not mention access control or data-protection terms.
- **Proof grade:** creator-claimed; no invoice or client is visible in the transcript.
