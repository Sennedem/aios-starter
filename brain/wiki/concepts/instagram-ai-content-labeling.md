# Instagram's wider "AI content" labelling

**Claim** (creator, 5:30-9:11 in [[wiki/sources/buildyourtribe-instagram-algorithm-2026|Build Your Tribe]]): Instagram now labels much more content as AI-generated than before, preferring to over-label rather than miss AI posts. The trigger anecdote: a creator's carousel that used Canva's generative background remover got an "AI" label. The host says labelling is not suppression ("they're not limiting your views"), though he concedes people may engage less with labelled posts.

**Grade: creator-empirical, unverified.** One anecdote; no Instagram document found. The "no distribution penalty" half is the less certain half: a label-driven drop in engagement is exactly the kind of effect that reduces reach without any explicit penalty.

## Why it matters

Tools with generative features (background removers, AI voices, generated stills) can trigger the label on content that is mostly human-made. Audience mood is, per the host, "very anti-AI", which makes a label a real cost even with no algorithmic penalty.

## Open question for any AI-assisted account

This source does not say whether a given tool's export carries the metadata that triggers the label. Test one post before relying on it.
