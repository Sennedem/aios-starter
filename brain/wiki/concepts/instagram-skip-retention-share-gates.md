# Instagram's three reel gates: skip rate, retention, share rate

The host of [[wiki/sources/buildyourtribe-instagram-algorithm-2026|Build Your Tribe]] frames reel distribution as three checks in sequence, using a nightclub analogy. The metrics are real Instagram Insights numbers; the sequence and the thresholds are the host's model, not published by Instagram.

| Gate | Club analogy | What it measures | Host's bar | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Skip rate | The bouncer | % of views that left within the first 3 seconds (Instagram's own definition) | 50% okay, 30% or less good, under 20% excellent | Definition official; bars creator-empirical (a third-party median of 60-65% contradicts the easy end) |
| 2. Retention | How good a night you have | Retention graph with no sharp drop-offs; % still watching at the end | Above 50% at the end | Creator-empirical |
| 3. Share (send) rate | VIP, on stage with the DJ | Shares or sends per reach | None given | Mosseri: sends per reach are "slightly more important" than likes for reaching strangers; "virality comes only from shares" is the host's overstatement |

The gate claim (a high skip rate stops Instagram from even looking at shares, likes and saves) is plausible folklore: the same unpublished "gate, then spread" story the YouTube side tells.

## Same shape as YouTube Shorts, different names

| Layer | YouTube Shorts (dmscales) | Instagram Reels (this source) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook, swipe-away | VVSA, about 80% floor ([[wiki/concepts/shorts-retention-vvsa-avd]]) | Skip rate, 3-second window, about 20-50% skip |
| Retention | AVD about 100% of length ([[wiki/concepts/shorts-retention-vvsa-avd]]) | Retention above 50% at the end |
| Spread | Seed audiences, group by group ([[wiki/concepts/seed-audience-testing]]) | Shares or sends per reach |

Differences worth keeping. The Instagram host sets a far looser retention bar (50% against about 100% AVD), likely because the two creators measure different things (percent still watching at the end against average view duration with rewatches) on different account sizes. They are not directly comparable, and neither is a platform number. Instagram names sends as the strongest stranger-reach signal; the YouTube Shorts material has no equivalent, so shares and sends are the one layer it does not teach.

## Cheapest use

Read a reel's Insights in the order skip rate, retention graph, shares. Only work on the next layer once the one before is acceptable. See [[wiki/techniques/instagram-reel-posting-checklist]].
