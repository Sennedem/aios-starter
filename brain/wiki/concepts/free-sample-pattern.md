# Free sample pattern (interactive tool before the contact form)

Give visitors a taste of the result before asking for anything: a quiz, a price estimator or a small useful tool, instead of explaining everything and then handing over a contact form. From [[wiki/sources/nateherk-10000-ai-websites|Nate Herk, I Studied 10,000 AI Websites]] (pattern 5, [09:14-10:58]).

## Why (creator's explanation)

The **IKEA effect** (Harvard and Duke researchers, creator-cited): people value things more when they helped make them. Entering their own answers makes the result feel like theirs, and like their own decision. Same logic as trying on clothes, where touching a product feels like owning it.

## Three forms Nate lists

1. A quiz ("which service is right for you", a hairstylist example).
2. A price estimator (Doc Braces, a Canadian orthodontist, with a braces cost calculator).
3. A useful tool: the Wise currency converter, Zillow's home-value estimate, Sherwin-Williams paint on a photo of your room.

## How to pick one

Find the question every customer asks before buying, choose a tool that answers it, and ask Claude to build it into the site. Keep it short and easy: it only works if people finish it.

## Evidence grade

The roughly 45% conversion for Doc Braces is quoted from Outgrow, which sells the calculator tool: a vendor figure, not independent, and not shown on screen. Treat it as creator-cited and vendor-sourced.
