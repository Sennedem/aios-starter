# Sell your judgment, not the thing you build

Nate Herk's one-sentence summary of his 13-model ranking: "the businesses at the bottom are where you're selling the thing you build, and the ones at the top are where you're selling your judgment." [13:05-13:14] From [[wiki/sources/nateherk-ai-businesses-ranked|Every AI Business Ranked]].

## The mechanism

AI makes building cheaper every month, so anything whose value is the artifact (an app, a website, a single automation) becomes a commodity. His examples: sites that agencies charge 5-10K for take an afternoon, and site builders sell them at about 9 USD a month; workflow builds fall to 500-2K and clients hire in-house; UGC video prices dropped about 44% in a year [03:35-07:01, 09:34].

What stays scarce is the part AI cannot do for the client:

- **Diagnosis**: finding the bottleneck and tying the fix to a business result ("think of it like being a doctor") [06:50, 11:13-11:17].
- **Change management**: governance, stakeholder buy-in, culture, getting people to actually change habits. "AI can write an audit, but it can't run the change inside of a company." [11:19-11:55]
- **Taste**: which model fits which audience, running ads and feeding the data back (creative studio) [09:38-09:44].
- **Trust built in the room**: workshops, where you see the problems and become "the AI expert in their lives" [08:01-08:12].

## How to apply it

When you score an offer, ask which side it sits on. A deliverable the client could soon generate themselves is the weak side. The diagnosis, the rollout and the accountability for a metric are the strong side. The practical sequence for moving up is in [[wiki/techniques/start-low-climb-to-judgment-businesses|Start low, climb to judgment businesses]].

## Caveat

Creator-argued, not tested. Nate gives no outcome data for consulting beating automation, and his own top entries (consulting, chief AI officer) need credibility a newcomer has not yet earned. It also points the opposite way from the software-first filters in [[wiki/concepts/three-filters-one-person-business|Three filters for a one-person business]]: those ask whether one person can run it, this asks whether it is a good business.
