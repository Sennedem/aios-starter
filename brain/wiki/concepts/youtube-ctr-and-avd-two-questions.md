# YouTube's two questions: will they click, will they watch

The long-form framing of the algorithm: for every video and every viewer the system asks (1) will this person click, and (2) if they click, will they watch? These map to two Studio numbers, **CTR** and **AVD**. For a small channel that wants to track as few numbers as possible, these are the two.

Source: [[wiki/sources/vidiq-youtube-algorithm-guide|vidIQ, "The Complete Guide to the YouTube Algorithm"]] (0:55-1:15).

## What the source adds

- **Retention is a percentage, watch time is a total.** 1,000 viewers times 5 minutes is 5,000 minutes of watch time; a 10-minute video where people leave at 4 minutes is 40% retention (3:33-3:57). A 6-minute video at 60% can beat a 12-minute video at 50%, because retention is compared against videos of similar topic and length (4:15-4:25). Creator-claimed; the comparison-to-peers mechanism is plausible, not documented.
- **Curve shapes diagnose the cause:** a steep drop in the first 30 seconds means the opening did not deliver what the thumbnail promised; a sudden mid-video cliff means a topic shift, a tangent or a pacing drop (3:58-4:12).
- **TV watch time has overtaken mobile**, and top-100 creator video length has "nearly doubled" (4:29-4:44). The source states this as YouTube-announced; not checked here.
- **The algorithm does not push videos to people; it finds videos for people** (1:20-1:30), attributed to YouTube engineers and Todd Beaupre.

## Grade

Corroborated in direction: YouTube's help page names clicks, watch time and satisfaction surveys as inputs. This video gives no specific thresholds, unlike the Shorts targets in [[wiki/concepts/shorts-retention-vvsa-avd|VVSA and AVD]].

## Link to the Shorts model

Shorts swap CTR for "viewed versus swiped away", because there is no thumbnail click; see [[wiki/concepts/shorts-retention-vvsa-avd|VVSA and AVD]]. Same question pair, different surface. Whether Shorts and long-form share one channel-level trust signal is unproven; see [[wiki/concepts/youtube-trust-score|YouTube trust score]].
