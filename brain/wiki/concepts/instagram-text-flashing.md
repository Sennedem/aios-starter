# "Text flashing" on Instagram reels

**Claim** (10:20-12:14 in [[wiki/sources/buildyourtribe-instagram-algorithm-2026|Build Your Tribe]]): reels that show a paragraph of text for about 2 seconds, too short to read, so viewers have to pause or rewatch, are now shown to fewer people, and Instagram "explicitly said" so.

**Grade: unverified.** No primary Instagram source found; blogs say Instagram shows mostly-text frames less but cite no document. The motive is coherent: the trick manufactured fake rewatches.

## The host's rule: read it aloud

If you cannot read the on-screen text out loud in the time it is displayed, it is on screen too briefly. Timed captions that follow the spoken voice are exempt, since they stay up as long as the words are spoken.

## Why it is cheap to follow regardless of grade

The rule costs nothing for a caption-led reel and protects against a plausible penalty. It conflicts with nothing in the YouTube Shorts material. If a reel has a hook card plus speech-synced captions, the check applies to the hook card, not the captions. See [[wiki/techniques/instagram-reel-posting-checklist]].
