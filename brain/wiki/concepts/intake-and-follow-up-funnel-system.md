# Intake and follow-up funnel system

System 2 of [[wiki/sources/nateherk-4-claude-systems-that-sell|Nate Herk's four]] **[02:11-04:41]**. It takes a lead from a social post to a paid, booked call without the owner chasing anyone.

- **Two parts:** (a) weekly multi-platform social posts in the owner's voice; (b) when someone fills in the interest form from a post: into the CRM, a text and an email with a payment link, after payment the intake form and waivers go out with reminders until signed, then a booking link with Zoom confirmation and follow-up reminders.
- **Buyer:** a semi-retired doctor who did not want a marketing team or a front desk. Nate's three-part buyer filter [03:29-03:48]: the business runs on bookings; leads arrive by form or phone and then sit; someone is chasing them by hand. All three true means it needs this.
- **Price:** $2,200, saving about 15 hours a week; the client rehired him for another $6,000 ("Luis"/"Lewis"). Found via Upwork [04:04-04:09]. The only one of the four with a repeat-purchase claim.
- **Pitch math:** 40 form fills last month, 10 paid, so go after the other 30; a few extra clients cover the fee [03:03-03:13]. It ties to revenue, "one of the easiest to pitch".
- **Sales process:** map first contact to booked call on one page, mark the steps that need a human, automate the rest, record a short demo and send it [03:51-04:03]. See [[wiki/techniques/pitch-ai-systems-on-business-outcome|the pitch technique]].
- **Caveat:** payment links and waivers are money and customer-data actions, so keep the human gate described in [[wiki/concepts/automation-boundary-human-in-the-loop|the automation boundary]]. It only helps demand-constrained firms (see the [[wiki/concepts/demand-vs-supply-constrained-business|constraint test]]).
- **Proof grade:** creator-claimed.
