# Instagram carousels outperform reels (claimed)

**Claim** (15:49-16:56 in [[wiki/sources/buildyourtribe-instagram-algorithm-2026|Build Your Tribe]]): carousels have dominated for months; in the host's community study, over 90 days carousels got 89% more views (1.9x) than reels.

**Reasoning offered:** supply and demand (everyone posts reels, few post carousels) and consumption style (slower, no sound needed, no pausing, scrolled at the viewer's own pace).

**Grade: creator-empirical, weak.** The study comes from his own paid community; sample, account sizes and method are not shown, and the host sells growth content, so he benefits from the carousel story. The supply-and-demand reasoning is plausible and untested. Do not treat 1.9x as a benchmark.

## How to test it

The cheapest test is a carousel version of a reel that already works (same payload), then compare reach. See [[wiki/techniques/instagram-reel-posting-checklist]].
