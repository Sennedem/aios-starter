# Creator OS system

System 3 of [[wiki/sources/nateherk-4-claude-systems-that-sell|Nate Herk's four]] **[04:41-06:32]**. Content in the creator's own voice and likeness, and the most natural path to monthly recurring revenue.

- **Problem:** a coach was producing all content by hand and could not keep up.
- **Build outline:** a cloned voice and likeness make narrated videos and on-brand images without her filming or writing. The builder also delivered extras: a 6-page site, a print-on-demand store, a 101-page guide (sold for about $200), a 9-email nurture sequence, SEO for 12 pages and a lead-capture quiz.
- **Buyer:** a coach or creator who already sells something with a proven back end.
- **Price:** about $3,600 up front, then $197/month. The client asked for the retainer before he offered one ("Charles"). Because of the bundled scope, the Claude-system share of the $3,600 is unknown.
- **Pitch angle is growth:** more content, more attention, more leads, more sales. Math: a $197 guide needs about 19 extra sales to cover $3,600 [05:56-06:05]. The weekly content need justifies the monthly fee. See [[wiki/techniques/pitch-ai-systems-on-business-outcome|the pitch technique]].
- **Outreach asset:** take 20 to 30 of the target's posts, build a voice profile, write three pieces and send "I built this for you, what do you think". If they like it, run one platform for a month, then sell the full build plus the monthly fee [06:12-06:32].
- **Fits:** demand-constrained businesses (see the [[wiki/concepts/demand-vs-supply-constrained-business|constraint test]]).
- **Risk to note:** a cloned voice and likeness raises consent and disclosure issues the video does not discuss.
- **Proof grade:** creator-claimed.
