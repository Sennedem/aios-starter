# Clay

A B2B lead-sourcing and enrichment platform, used as the lead-sourcing leg of the [[wiki/concepts/one-person-business-three-part-architecture|3-part one-person business architecture]] in [[wiki/sources/nateherk-one-person-business-claude|Nate Herk's one-person business video]]. **[07:36-08:20]** Clay is a paid sponsor of that video, so the claims below are the source's own framing, not independently verified.

## What it's claimed to do

- **Finds businesses that match a specific signal.** In the worked example: companies publicly deploying AI agents.
- **Pulls B2B data other tools and agents can't reach**, described as "the best B2B data out there", needed to build "a high-quality list of decision-makers that actually fit your ICP."
- **Enriches leads** so messages can be personalized at scale.
- **Can write the personalized outreach and schedule the sending itself.** In the worked example Claude wrote the messages from Clay's enriched data instead, but the source says Clay can do that step too and calls it "a one-stop shop."

## How it was actually used

Clay surfaced the candidate companies. Claude checked public sources, explained why each company was a fit and drafted the outreach message. The draft stayed marked **not sent**, pending the founder's review (see [[wiki/concepts/automation-boundary-human-in-the-loop|the automation boundary]]). The source is explicit that finding a relevant company and writing a good message "is not the same as getting a customer": the unproven step is sending, getting replies and seeing who pays.

In a later video, [[wiki/sources/nateherk-ai-businesses-ranked|Every AI business ranked]], Nate cites Clay passing $100M in annual revenue as evidence that AI lead generation is a durable business (creator-cited, source not shown).

## Caveat

Because Clay sponsors the video, treat "best B2B data out there" and "one-stop shop" as marketing, not as a comparison checked against Apollo, Clearbit or similar tools. The source gives no pricing, setup steps or independent evaluation.
