# Source: "NEW Instagram Algorithm: How To Go Viral And Get Followers" by Build Your Tribe

A 17-minute YouTube episode, published 2026-10-05, presenting six "algorithm updates for Q4 2026". The host signs off "Brock"; the channel sells Instagram growth content and the "Insta Club Hub" community.

**Source grading:** creator-empirical, not Instagram-official. Mixed incentives: the host promotes Metricool with a discount code (9:43-10:12, an affiliate-style plug), cites a study from his own paid community (16:41), and teases a follow-up trial-reels video (13:53, 15:31). He cites Adam Mosseri for three of the six points, and the Mosseri-attributed ones are the best supported (see table). No primary Instagram or Mosseri document could be found for text flashing, duplicate trial reels or the AI-label change, only third-party write-ups, so those stay "creator-claimed".

## Claims, graded

Grades: **Official** (Instagram or Mosseri stated, confirmed by more than the host), **Corroborated** (independent sources agree), **Creator-empirical** (the host's observation, plausible, unverified), **Folklore**, **Unverified** (could not be checked; do not plan on it).

## What the source adds

- An Instagram algorithm model with the same three-layer shape as the YouTube Shorts set (a hook gate, then retention, then a spread signal) under Instagram-specific metric names. Cross-platform comparison in [[wiki/concepts/instagram-skip-retention-share-gates]].
- A posting checklist: [[wiki/techniques/instagram-reel-posting-checklist]].
- Disagrees with the dmscales folklore on whether pausing posting hurts (claim 8).
- Does not cover hashtags, posting time, captions or SEO, and says nothing about cold start for a brand-new account. It describes mature-account behaviour only.
