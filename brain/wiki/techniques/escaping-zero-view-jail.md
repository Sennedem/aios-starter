# Escaping zero view jail (new-channel setup)

What to do before posting so a new channel clears YouTube's bot screen and gets a [[wiki/concepts/seed-audience-testing|seed audience]] at all, instead of landing in [[wiki/concepts/zero-view-jail|zero view jail]].

Source: [[wiki/sources/dmscales-shorts-algorithm|dmscales, "The Entire Shorts Algorithm Explained in 180 Seconds"]] (00:01-00:24). In the narration: "most importantly, enable third level feature eligibility. This is normally the make or break for getting view jail because it instantly proves you're a real person."

## Checklist, in the source's order of importance

1. **Enable advanced ("third level") feature eligibility.** Called the make-or-break, because it verifies that a real person is behind the account. In YouTube Studio: Settings, then Channel, then Feature eligibility, and complete the advanced-features verification (phone verification unlocks intermediate features; ID or video verification, or established channel history, unlocks advanced). This is the one item that is a documented, verifiable YouTube setting rather than inferred mechanics. It also gates upload volume and channel features, so do it whatever you think of the rest of the model.
2. **Use an aged channel** rather than one created minutes ago.
3. **Use an older email with real history** behind the account.

Items 2 and 3 are graded as unverifiable account-farming lore in [[wiki/concepts/youtube-trust-score|the trust score grading]]. They cost nothing if you already have an older account; do not buy or farm one for this.

## Why this order makes sense

The screen is an identity check, not a quality check. Age and history are weak circumstantial signals; verification is a direct one. If only one item gets done, make it the first.

## What this does not fix

Clearing the bot screen only buys you distribution. Whether the seed audience watches is a separate problem governed by [[wiki/concepts/shorts-retention-vvsa-avd|retention]] and content quality; see [[wiki/techniques/diagnosing-a-flatlined-short|diagnosing a flatlined short]].
