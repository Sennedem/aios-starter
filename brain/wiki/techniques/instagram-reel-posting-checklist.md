# Instagram reel posting checklist

Run it before posting and again when reading a reel's Insights. Built from [[wiki/sources/buildyourtribe-instagram-algorithm-2026|Build Your Tribe]]; each line carries its evidence grade so you know what is a rule and what is a bet.

## Before posting

1. **Hook for the 3-second window.** The first 3 seconds decide skip rate. Get the payload on screen or spoken inside it. *Evidence: official metric, creator advice.*
2. **Read-aloud test on every on-screen text block.** If you cannot say it in the time it shows, lengthen it or cut it. Speech-synced captions are exempt. *Evidence: unverified penalty, free to follow.* See [[wiki/concepts/instagram-text-flashing]].
3. **Remove other apps' watermarks.** Editing in CapCut or InShot is fine; a visible foreign watermark is the one thing that does hurt. Your own logo is fine. *Evidence: Mosseri, via secondary sources.*
4. **Do not reuse a reel with only the text changed** (relevant if you test with trial reels). Vary the script, the spoken line or the scene. *Evidence: creator-observed.* See [[wiki/concepts/instagram-trial-reels-duplicate-detection]].
5. **Send test.** Name who would send this to whom, and why. *Evidence: Mosseri names sends per reach as the strongest signal for reaching strangers; the "3-5x a like" multiplier is unverified.*
6. **Scheduling is safe.** Use a scheduler freely. *Evidence: Mosseri, via an aggregator.*
7. **AI-assisted assets** may get an "AI" label from generative tools. Test on one post. *Evidence: single anecdote.* See [[wiki/concepts/instagram-ai-content-labeling]].

## Reading Insights, in order

1. **Skip rate.** Compare against your own account's median, not the host's 20, 30 and 50% bars (a third-party median reads 60-65%). Fix hooks first.
2. **Retention graph.** Find sharp drops and the second where viewers leave. Aim for above 50% at the end (a creator bar).
3. **Share or send rate per reach.** Only now optimise for sends.

## Test, do not assume

- Post one carousel version of a working reel to see whether the 1.9x story holds for your account ([[wiki/concepts/instagram-carousel-advantage]]).
- Posting gaps: the host says there is no penalty, dmscales says a gap re-jails you. Both are unverified; your own account's numbers before and after a gap are the only evidence ([[wiki/concepts/youtube-trust-score]]).
