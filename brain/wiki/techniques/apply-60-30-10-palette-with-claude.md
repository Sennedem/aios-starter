# Apply a 60-30-10 palette with Claude

From [[wiki/sources/nateherk-10000-ai-websites|Nate Herk, I Studied 10,000 AI Websites]] [03:27-04:18]. Concept: [[wiki/concepts/highlighter-rule-60-30-10|Highlighter rule]].

1. Choose three colours: main (60%, background), secondary (30%, text, menu, cards, sections), accent (10%). Nate's living-room example: pale grey, navy, burnt orange.
2. If you do not want to choose, take a ready palette from a site like Coolors and copy the hex codes.
3. Paste the three codes into Claude and instruct it: use **only** these colours, follow the 60-30-10 rule, accent only on buttons, links and a few specific words.
4. Check the result: is the button the single most eye-catching element? If the accent also sits on headlines, icons or borders, tell Claude to remove it there.

Nate gives no exact prompt wording. Steps 1 to 3 are his description; the check in step 4 is the direct test the highlighter metaphor implies.
