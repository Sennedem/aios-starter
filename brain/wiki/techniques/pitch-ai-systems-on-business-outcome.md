# Pitch AI systems on business outcome

Nate Herk's repeatable pitch recipe across the four systems in [[wiki/sources/nateherk-4-claude-systems-that-sell|4 Claude Systems That Sell]] **[01:07-01:25, 03:03-03:13, 05:56-06:05, 07:17-07:31, 09:34-09:46]**.

1. **Diagnose the constraint first** ([[wiki/concepts/demand-vs-supply-constrained-business|demand vs supply]]).
2. **Ask one question the owner can answer with a number**, then multiply:
   - [[wiki/concepts/voice-note-to-document-system|Voice notes]]: foremen x hours of paperwork a week (5 x 5 = 25 paid hours).
   - [[wiki/concepts/intake-and-follow-up-funnel-system|Intake funnel]]: form fills last month vs payers (40 vs 10; chase the other 30).
   - [[wiki/concepts/creator-os-system|Creator OS]]: price of their product vs your fee (a $197 guide; about 19 extra sales covers $3,600).
   - [[wiki/concepts/company-brain-system|Company brain]]: team size x hours lost searching (20 x 2 = 40 payroll hours a week).
3. **Add the costs beyond the fee:** time, risk (safety, compliance), change management. The sticker price is only part of what the owner weighs [01:41-01:55].
4. **Lead with the outcome, not the tech.** "A business growth person who happens to use AI when AI is the right fit" [09:41].
5. **Prove it cheaply before the sale:** a short recorded demo (funnel), three pieces in their voice (creator), a system built for yourself first (company brain).
6. **Start with one document, one question set, one platform**, and expand after it works. A retainer follows (monthly plans in the creator and company-brain cases).

Caveat: the pitch math produces hypothetical savings, not measured ones. The video's only measured result is "about 15 hours a week" for the funnel.
