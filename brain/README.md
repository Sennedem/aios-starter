# Brain

This is your second brain: everything Claude knows about you, your work and what you have learned, as plain markdown. Open this folder in Obsidian to browse it. Claude reads this index first to find the right page, so every content page gets one line here.

Rules for links and frontmatter are in [[conventions]].

## Context (who you are and what matters now)

- [[context/about-me|About me]]: identity, situation, background, constraints
- [[context/work|Work]]: your businesses, job and studies at a glance, one paragraph each
- [[context/how-i-work|How I work]]: schedule, tools, preferences, how Claude should behave with you
- [[context/priorities|Priorities]]: what matters this quarter, ranked
- [[context/network|Network]]: the people you work with and who they are

## Projects (one folder per business, job, course or big project)

- [[projects/README|Projects]]: the hub. `/setup` creates one `CONTEXT.md` per project.

## Decisions

- [[decisions/log|Decision log]]: append-only, every real decision with its why

## References (frameworks and notes you reuse)

- [[references/README|References]]: the hub

## Wiki (what you learn from outside sources)

- [[wiki/README|Wiki]]: videos, articles and PDFs filed by `/ingest` into small linked pages
- [[wiki/log|Ingest log]]: append-only

## Working areas

- [[brainstorms/README|Brainstorms]]: raw captures from `/grill-me` and `/setup`
- [[audits/README|Audits]]: weekly `/audit` reports and the janitor report
